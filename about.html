Why Packdez exists

Most teams don't need another SaaS tool. They need their existing systems to work better.

Growing teams hit the same wall: spreadsheets that worked at 10 people break at 50. Manual processes that took 2 hours now take 8. Data lives in 5 different tools, and someone spends their week copying it between them.

This friction is solvable. Google Sheets is already where teams work. Apps Script and integrations can automate most repetitive work without forcing a platform migration or months of implementation.

Packdez builds operational systems that are reliable, documented, and designed to scale — so teams can stop managing spreadsheets and start running their business.
