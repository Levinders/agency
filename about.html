ABOUT PACKDEZ

We build systems teams can rely on.

Packdez helps teams turn Google Sheets into reliable, automated systems — removing manual work, reducing errors, and creating clarity where spreadsheets usually break.

Why Packdez exists

Most teams don’t need more tools — they need their existing systems to work.

Google Sheets is powerful, but without structure and automation it becomes fragile. Formulas break. Data gets copied manually. Reports are rebuilt instead of trusted.

Packdez was created to fix that gap: building operational systems that are simple to use, hard to break, and designed to scale with the team using them.

How Packdez works

Every project follows a predictable, low-friction process.

  • • We start by mapping how work actually happens today
  • • We automate only what creates real leverage
  • • We build directly inside Google Sheets using Apps Script and APIs
  • • We document everything so teams own the system

The result is a system your team understands, trusts, and can run without constant fixes.

Who’s behind Packdez

Packdez is led by Raphael Levinder, a systems builder focused on operational automation, reporting, and internal tools.

Over the past years, Raphael has worked with founders, operations teams, and agencies to replace manual workflows with reliable Google Sheets systems — often removing entire categories of repetitive work.

Clients consistently highlight clarity, communication, and systems that work as promised — not prototypes that break under pressure.
